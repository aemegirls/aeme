Un trabajo diferente

for
AgroBank

AgroBank faces the challenge of encouraging generational renewal in Spain's agricultural sector, making farming and livestock feel like an attractive, innovative and future-oriented career for young people.

Our campaign's aim was to emotionally connect with a new generation that strives to avoid monotony. We showed that to lead a different life, you should start with a different job.

‹
We wanted farming to feel less like choosing a job and more like choosing a different way of life.
