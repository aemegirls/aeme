El Tren de la Feria

el Sol x Andalucía

This brief mentions Andalusia as being overcrowded. But there are parts of Andalusia that are quite the opposite…

So we decided to do something about it. A train that takes tourists through lesser-known towns in Andalusia so they can enjoy their festivals and traditions and be immersed in Andalusian culture.

We changed a few ideas in the film... And thank God we did, because we ended up on the train! ✦ ✧
