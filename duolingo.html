In Argentina, massive summer blackouts cut off internet access for millions at a time… that's why we turned the dead end into a learning moment.

404 Lesson Found hijacked the offline experience: when connection drops, an API detects the Duolingo app and replaces Chrome's iconic Dino with Duo, unlocking vocabulary and grammar lessons that work entirely offline.
